# Reason code: product_not_received
The customer paid but claims the goods or service never arrived.
## Required evidence
- Proof of delivery (courier tracking with a delivered status and timestamp).
- Delivery address matching the order.
- Signed receipt or photo proof of delivery where available.
- Customer communication acknowledging receipt.
## Notes
A delivered-status tracking record to the order address is decisive. Digital
goods require an access/download log instead.
